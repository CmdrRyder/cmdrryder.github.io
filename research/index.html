Research

I study unsteady flows next to moving bodies, walls and interfaces, using volumetric optical diagnostics, refractive-index matching and pressure reconstruction, and I build the facilities and methods these measurements need.

Each project has its own page with an overview, figures and the papers behind it.

Boundary-aware reconstruction

LE-DM turns scattered particle tracks into velocity fields that respect moving solid boundaries.

Freely moving bodies

Flow around spheres rising under buoyancy, imaged in 3D through a refractive-index-matched liquid.
