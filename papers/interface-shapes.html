Interface deformation around particles

Measurements and modeling of the liquid–gas interface around particles of anisotropic shape.

PublishedJournal of Fluid Mechanics

Overview

Particles at fluid interfaces change the surrounding surface geometry. This study examines how anisotropic particle shape influences the resulting interface deformation.

Experimental approach

I built a fluorescence-based laser-sheet scanning system with stereo imaging that maps the interface with 0.1 mm precision. The measurements test a closed-form solution, based on domain perturbations, for the interface around particles of complex shape with a pinned contact line.

Key result

Measurements around 3D-printed circular, polygonal and undulated particles agree with the model.
