Academic teaching

Teaching & mentoring

I teach fluid mechanics and aerospace engineering through a combination of physical reasoning, prediction, measurement, and critical evaluation of results.

Courses and laboratory instruction

Technion, 2025–2026. Advanced Measurement Techniques in Fluid Mechanics. Led the PIV module and taught Schlieren instruction in 2025, training a new Ph.D. student to take over Schlieren in 2026.

Johns Hopkins University, 2015–2018. Teaching assistant for Thermodynamics and Jet & Rocket Propulsion, including recitations, office hours, assignments, and assessment.

IIT Kanpur, 2013–2014. Teaching assistant for graduate Aerodynamics and Aerospace Engineering Laboratory.

Mentorship

At the Technion I have mentored three M.Sc. students, one Ph.D. student and undergraduate researchers in experimental fluid mechanics, diagnostics and data analysis, within the laboratory's formal supervision structure.

Teaching interests

Fluid mechanics, aerodynamics, thermodynamics, propulsion, heat transfer, multiphase flows and experimental measurement techniques.
