Shock-wave propagation through expansions

Experiments and numerical analysis reveal how a shock wave develops far downstream of an abrupt duct-area expansion.

PublishedJournal of Fluid Mechanics

Overview

Abrupt changes in duct geometry cause transmitted shocks to deform, reflect, and interact with the developing flow. This work investigates how the front recovers downstream.

Experimental approach

Shock-tube experiments, schlieren imaging, and complementary numerical simulations.

Key result

The study develops a predictive description of shock-front evolution downstream of abrupt area expansions.

Publication

The dynamics of shock wave propagation far downstream of an abrupt area expansion
